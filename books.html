Books

Releasing September 10, 2026 · World Suicide Prevention Day

Some days, breathing feels like the hardest thing you’ll do. This is a practical, no-fluff guide for the moments your mind won’t settle — designed to support you through intrusive thoughts, panic, paranoia, grief, and the quiet weight of depression.

Written by a licensed clinical social worker with real clinical training and lived experience, this workbook pairs professional-level tools with real-world guidance that provides practical, third-grade-reading-level support.

Get Notified at Launch
Inside the Workbook

What You’ll Find

  • Grounding and mindfulness skills for acute distress
  • CBT and DBT tools built for real-time, in-the-moment use
  • A step-by-step guide to your own safety plan
  • Support for intrusive thoughts, psychosis, and passive suicidal ideation
  • A clear map of the mental health system — outpatient therapy, EAP, support groups, and finding a therapist
  • Guidance on inpatient hospitalization and patient rights
  • Music- and art-based regulation pages
  • A companion 52-card skills deck (available separately)
