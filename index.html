About Dr. Christy

A decade of meeting people in crisis and helping them find the next step.

Dr. Christiana Redeaux is a licensed clinical social worker, educator, and mental health advocate. Her work focuses on supporting individuals during moments of crisis and helping them navigate care access in a way that feels practical and manageable.
