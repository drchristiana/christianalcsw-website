What is Redeaux & Co.?

Tools I wish I’d had

Redeaux & Co. is the home for everything I make that you can print, hold, or wear. That includes my books, printable tools for clinicians and the people they serve, and a few everyday pieces with a message.

Redeaux is my family name, and the fleur-de-lis is a nod to my Louisiana roots. I put my name on it because every product is written or reviewed by me, a licensed clinical social worker. If it has the Redeaux & Co. name on it, I stand behind it.
